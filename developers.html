Future — not built yet

Future Developer Mode.

Developer Mode is potential future infrastructure. None of it exists in V1, and none of it is promised on a date. Contemplated capabilities include:

Authenticated APIs

First-class programmatic access with proper credentials, quotas, and audit trails.

Programmatic mail

Applications composing and sending correspondence through supported interfaces.

Webhooks

Event notifications for deliveries and mailbox activity an application is authorized to see.

Verified organizational senders

Known projects and organizations sending as verified identities, clearly distinguished from strangers.

Application-generated correspondence

Product and service mail generated by applications on behalf of verified senders.

Project-to-holder communication

Verified projects reaching their holders — opt-in, separated from personal mail.

Every item above is future work. If you are evaluating $MAIL today, evaluate what is published: the V1 source, its documentation, and its tests.
