Roadmap

Sequence,
not schedule.

What exists, what comes next, and what remains research. No dates, no completion percentages — the order is the commitment.

Current

V1 foundation and public technical source. The consumer mailbox — letters, requests, junk, blocking, reporting, privacy-first share cards — with signature authentication, published security documentation, and a rerunnable adversarial test harness.

Next

Discovery + Mail Flag + opt-in notifications + stronger abuse infrastructure. Privacy-safe ways for wallet owners to learn that mail is waiting, notification channels owners explicitly enroll, and deeper anti-spam and abuse protection ahead of broader adoption.

Later

Developer Mode + APIs + early Mail Desk. Authenticated programmatic access, and the first project-facing surface for verified senders — strictly separated from personal mail and opt-in per project.

Research

Packages, contextual airdrops, Certified Mail, asset-aware correspondence, and future network/token economics. Value-bearing mail will not be built until a dedicated security architecture has been reviewed and approved — and basic consumer mail stays free regardless.

The fixed principle

Basic consumer mail should remain free. Future token utility should come from legitimate network demand and advanced infrastructure — not forced access to basic communication.

The long-form version of this roadmap, with the reasoning behind it, is in the whitepaper.
