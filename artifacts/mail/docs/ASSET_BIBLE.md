# $MAIL ASSET BIBLE

## Master Visual Identity
The canonical visual identity is established by `mailbox-hero.webp`.
- **Subject**: A stylized, 3D cartoon mailbox character.
- **Composition**: Mailbox mounted on a silver metal post. Usually slightly off-center for UI balance.
- **Colors**:
  - Body: Glossy deep purple.
  - Face: Flat/matte light purple.
  - Features: Bright pink smiling mouth (U-shape), bright pink flag.
  - Eyes: Large white ovals with black pupils.
- **Lighting & Material**: Soft, studio-style 3D lighting. Glossy highlights on the purple body and pink flag to feel tactile and plastic/ceramic.
- **Background**: Always transparent.
- **Forbidden Deviations**: 
  - No generic crypto mascots (no Shiba Inus, no monkeys).
  - No floating coins or blockchain grids.
  - Do not change the color palette (no blue/green mailboxes).
  - Keep the face language simple (big eyes, U-shaped mouth).

## Limitations of Current Source Asset
- The current `mailbox-hero.webp` has the pink flag in the "UP" position baked into the flattened raster image.
- Because it cannot be cleanly separated at professional quality via code, we use it as the default representation and use UI interactions to imply "new mail" rather than mutilating the asset.
