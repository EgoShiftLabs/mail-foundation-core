# $MAIL — Future Roadmap

V1 ships the mailbox world, signature auth, letters, requests, junk,
blocking, reports, and privacy-first share cards. What comes later:

## The Porch (V1.x)

The boarded-up porch opens: a public drop-off where anyone can leave a
note visible to visitors of that mailbox. Owner moderates their porch.
Placeholder already exists in the world so the reveal feels earned.

## Mail Desk (V2)

The project-facing surface: verified projects send signed announcements
to their holders. Strictly separated from personal mail; recipients
opt in per project. This is deliberately absent from V1 so personal
correspondence defines the product first.

## Postage & scarcity (V2)

Optional paid postage for reaching wallets outside approved
correspondence — an economic spam filter that keeps plain replies free.

## Enclosures (V2+)

Letters that carry something: images, then tokens/NFT enclosures with
explicit, transaction-signing acceptance (never automatic).

## Identity & trust (continuous)

- SNS/.sol name resolution for addressing and display
- Sender reputation signals from accept/junk/report ratios
- Verified badges for known entities

## Notifications (V1.x)

Wallet-native and push notification of new deliveries ("the flag is up")
without leaking sender or content off-platform.

## Multi-mailbox (V2+)

One person, several wallets: a unified doorstep that keeps per-wallet
privacy boundaries intact.
